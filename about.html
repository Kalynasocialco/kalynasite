About Kalyna Social Co.

Rooted in where we come from.

Why "Kalyna"

A berry, a symbol, a thread back home.

Kalyna, the viburnum berry, is the national symbol of Ukraine. It shows up in folk songs, embroidery, and family stories, representing home, resilience, and roots that hold even when life moves you somewhere new.

Our founder was raised by Ukrainian immigrant parents who built a life in Canada from scratch. As their first-born Canadian daughter, she grew up between two worlds, and the name Kalyna became a way of carrying that heritage forward into something new of her own.

That's the spirit behind this agency: tend something carefully, give it real structure, and it keeps growing season after season. That's what we believe good social media should do for your business too.

"Running a business is a full-time job on its own. Marketing shouldn't be another one."

What we believe

Social media as a real business tool, not just another task.

Strategy before content

Every strategy starts with knowing who you are and what you're trying to achieve, then builds from there. Nothing gets posted just to fill a calendar.

Consistency builds trust

Showing up reliably is what brings people through the door. We handle the publishing schedule so it never slips, no matter how busy your season gets.

Real measurement, real adjustment

Every month, we check what's actually working and adjust. The strategy keeps getting sharper, never stale, never set-and-forget.

Background

Marketing, communications, and digital strategy, across non-profits and local business.

Before Kalyna Social Co., our founder spent time working in marketing, communications, and digital strategy across non-profit organizations and local businesses. That mix matters: non-profits teach you to communicate with purpose on tight resources, and local business teaches you what actually drives someone to pick up the phone.

That combination shaped how we work now, treating every client's social media like a real growth channel, with the same rigor you'd expect from an in-house marketing hire, minus the overhead of hiring one.
