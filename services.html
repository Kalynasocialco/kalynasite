  • A heads-up on promotions or seasonal changes

  • Revisions are capped at two rounds per post unless agreed otherwise
  • Anything outside your package gets quoted separately
  • Ad spend isn't included in the monthly fee
  • We can't guarantee results, platforms control their own algorithms
  • We just need 14 days' notice to pause or cancel
