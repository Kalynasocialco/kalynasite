Our Process

01

Discovery & Alignment

We'll hop on a call to talk through your business, your audience, and what you're hoping to achieve.

02

Positioning & Planning

We map out your content pillars, nail down your messaging, and build themes tied to your offers and the season ahead.

03

Content Creation & Approval

We create the content, you review it, and we finalize everything on a clear approval schedule.

04

Implementation & Engagement

Content goes live, gets monitored, and engagement is handled based on whatever package you're on.

05

Reporting & Optimization

Every month, we look at what's working, fine-tune the messaging, and keep pushing toward real, measurable growth.

What we'll need from you

  • Your brand assets: logo, fonts, colours
  • Timely access to your social platforms
  • Content approvals within 48 hours
  • A heads-up on promotions or seasonal changes
  • A quick response if something needs your input

A few things to know

  • Revisions are capped at two rounds per post unless agreed otherwise
  • Anything outside your package gets quoted separately
  • Ad spend isn't included in the monthly fee
  • We can't guarantee results, platforms control their own algorithms
  • We just need 14 days' notice to pause or cancel
