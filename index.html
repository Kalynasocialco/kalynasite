The name

Kalyna is the national berry of Ukraine.

It's a symbol of home, resilience, and roots, passed down through generations. Our founder, Anastasia Chernous, was raised by Ukrainian immigrant parents, first-born Canadian, with the name as a constant thread back to where her family began.

This business carries that name on purpose. The same way Kalyna represents continuity and care across generations, we treat your business's presence online as something worth tending carefully: built to last, not just to trend.
